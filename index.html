A note about this website

This site was built with the help of Artificial Intelligence. I'm an Aeronautical Engineering graduate, not a coder, and AI helped me turn an idea into something usable for fellow AELE reviewees.

I know AI use isn't environmentally free, and I don't want to brush past that. But I believe how and why we use a tool matters as much as whether we use it at all. Here, it went toward making review materials easier to access and organize.

“Maybe it's less about AI being good or bad, and more about the purpose we choose to use it for, while still acknowledging its impact on our environment and starting the change with ourselves to help protect it.”

You may forget today what you studied yesterday, and you may forget tomorrow what you studied today, and that is okay. Think of every lesson you study as someone you’ve just met along the way. 💬 A NOTE FROM THE CREATOR ▼

This platform was created from a simple hope: to make AELE preparation a little more accessible, organized, and manageable for those working toward the same goal. It is being developed into a more comprehensive review platform, with structured lessons, detailed explanations, and interactive animations to help make difficult concepts, especially in Aerodynamics, easier to understand, visualize, and remember.

The platform is also meant to help reviewees become more comfortable with answering exams in a computerized environment. Since the AELE is conducted through a Computer-Based Licensure Examination (CBLE) setup, I hope that practicing in a similar format can make the experience a little less unfamiliar when the actual examination day comes.

If you find this platform helpful in your AELE preparation and would like to support the project, visiting the Flight Store is one way you can help. Every purchase contributes to keeping the platform running and gives me the opportunity to continue improving it, adding new features, and creating more resources for future examinees.

There is absolutely no obligation to purchase anything. Simply using the reviewer, sharing it with a fellow reviewee, or leaving feedback already means more than you might realize. Your feedback helps me understand how this platform can become better and more useful for the people who need it.

If you have your own review materials or mock exams that you would be willing to share, I would also be grateful to have them become part of this growing collection. Feel free to contribute a file, and I’ll work on getting it published. The goal is to build a place where future examinees can find mock exams and review materials from different sources, so no one has to feel like they are preparing alone or searching everywhere for something to practice with.

Thank you for taking the time to use this platform, for supporting it in whatever way you can, and for being part of its journey. I hope it can be even a small help as you work toward the day you can finally call yourself a licensed Aeronautical Engineer.

Thank you, and best of luck to all future Aeronautical Engineers ✈️!

          • 📌 Pace yourself within each subject’s time limit. Some carry more weight and more hours than others.
